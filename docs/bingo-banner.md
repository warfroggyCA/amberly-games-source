# Bingo celebration

A newly observed saved Scrabble turn marked `bingo` shows a green-and-gold celebration for the scorer and live viewers: individually raised ivory BINGO! tiles, a metallic +50 bonus medallion, double gold framing and a brief sparkle burst. It identifies the player and saved turn total and states that the 50-point bonus is already included. The scoring engine remains authoritative; the banner never changes a score.

The banner dismisses after eight seconds or through its labelled 44px close button. It does not grab focus or block board input, and all entrance, tile and sparkle motion is disabled for reduced-motion users. The celebration sits above the existing score-pop effect so its text remains readable. Initial history loads, refreshes, drafts, repeated polls, and previously seen turn IDs do not trigger it. Undo or a subsequent turn clears it. A seven-letter word made using fewer than seven new tiles is not a bingo.

Validation: TypeScript, targeted ESLint/format checks and production build passed. All 16 focused browser cases passed across desktop Chromium, iPhone WebKit portrait/landscape and iPad WebKit emulation. Scorer and viewer cases cover saved-only display, dismissal, undo, refresh/replay suppression, expiry during polling, non-bingo extensions, reduced motion and viewport fit. Rendered screenshots inspected; physical-device and hosted acceptance remain separate. No deployment or hosted records were changed for this feature.
