# Provisional live entry

The shared scorer can show their in-progress placements and potential score to signed-in observers and private-link viewers. The server calculates the provisional score and validity with that game’s lexicon, verified words, rack count and physical tile supply. Official scores, crowns, bag totals, records, word authorship and turn animations still use confirmed game state exclusively.

Transport is near-live, not a promise of simultaneous delivery: changed placements are coalesced to at most four serialized requests per second; lightweight spectator draft polls run once per second while visible. Private viewers refresh full game history every five seconds, or immediately when the lightweight response reports a changed turn revision or scorer generation. No entire history is sent per keystroke. A visible scorer renews an unchanged entry every four seconds; abandoned previews expire after twelve seconds. Connection failures preserve local entries and cannot save a score.

The private `scrabble.live_drafts` table holds at most one replaceable row per game, outside journal events, results, audit and history exports. A clear is a sequenced empty preview, so late earlier requests cannot revive it. Drafts are bound to game revision, scorer generation and player. Only the current verified scorer account may write, irrespective of device. New actual edits can supersede another device’s preview; idle heartbeats, lost-response retries and old-page clears cannot take it back. Confirming a turn, pausing/ending a game or transferring the scorer invalidates the prior preview.

The account endpoint checks the authenticated identity and family membership on every request. The private viewing token grants read access only; its hash, expiry, revocation and creating member’s current access are checked for lightweight and full reads. Anonymous Supabase roles have no access to the table or its projection functions. Draft placements are limited to seven unique board squares; no caller-provided potential score is accepted.
